# FoodDesk Support Guide for Staff & Owners

This guide explains how to use the FoodDesk platform to manage your restaurant operations.

## 1. Welcome to FoodDesk
FoodDesk (formerly Qserves) is your all-in-one restaurant management system. Use the Admin Dashboard to control everything from your menu to your table orders.

## 2. Managing Orders
### How to take an order:
- **Staff/Waiters**: Use the "Save Order" screen to select a table and add items. 
- **Customers**: They can scan the QR code on their table to open the menu and place orders directly from their phones.
- **Tracking**: Use the "Orders" page to see current orders and their status (Pending, Preparing, Ready, or Completed).

### How to update order status:
- In the "Orders" or "Kitchen" view, click on an order to change its status as it moves from the kitchen to the table.

## 3. Billing & Payments
### How to generate a bill:
1. Go to the "Orders" page.
2. Select the active order for a table.
3. Click "Generate Bill" to finalize the items and total.
4. Once the customer pays, select the payment method (Cash, UPI, or Card) and mark it as "Completed."

### How to get a PDF invoice:
- After an order is completed, you can click the "Download Bill" button to get a professional PDF receipt that you can print or send to the customer.

## 4. Setting Up Your Tables & QR Codes
### How to add a new table:
1. Go to "Store Management."
2. Click "Add Table" and enter the table number.
3. The system will automatically generate a unique QR code for that table.

### How to get your QR code stickers:
- In "Store Management," you can see the QR codes for all your tables. You can print these and place them on your restaurant tables for customers to scan.

## 5. Adding Menu Items
### How to add food/drinks:
1. Go to the "Inventory" or "Menu" page.
2. Use the "Add Product" button to enter the name, price, and category.
3. You can also use the "Menu Scanner" to take a photo of your printed menu, and the AI will help you add all items at once.

## 6. Subscriptions & Account
### What to do if you are blocked:
- If you see a "Subscription Expired" message, go to the "Subscription" page to renew your plan. You must have an active plan to access orders and operational features.

## 7. Getting Extra Help
- If you have an issue that isn't covered here, click the **"Human Support"** button in the chat. This will connect you directly with our support team on WhatsApp.
